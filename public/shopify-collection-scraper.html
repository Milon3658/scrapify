What Data Gets Extracted from Shopify Collections?

Product Title & Handle

Get the full product name and URL-safe handle (slug) for every product in the Shopify collection.

Price & Compare Price

Extract current selling price and compare-at price (original MRP) to calculate discount percentage.

Variants (Size, Color, etc.)

Extract all product variants including size, color, material with their individual prices and SKUs.

Product Images

Get all product image URLs at full resolution for every item in the collection.

Inventory & SKU

Extract SKU codes, inventory quantity, and fulfillment type for each product variant.

Tags & Vendor

Get product tags, vendor name, and product type metadata for filtering and categorization.

How to Scrape a Shopify Collection in 3 Steps

  1. Open any Shopify collection page

    Navigate to a collection like https://yourstore.myshopify.com/collections/all or any product category URL.

  2. Paste URL into Scrapify, select Category Listing mode

    Scrapify auto-detects Shopify stores and uses the built-in /products.json endpoint for maximum accuracy.

  3. Download or export your product data

    Download as JSON or export as CSV to import into Google Sheets, Excel, or your own database.
