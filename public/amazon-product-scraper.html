How to Scrape Amazon Products in 3 Steps

  1. Copy the Amazon Product URL

    Open any Amazon product page (e.g. https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08N5WRWNW) and copy the URL from your browser.

  2. Paste into Scrapify & Select Mode

    Paste the URL into Scrapify, select "Single Product Detail Page" mode from the dropdown, and click Scrapify Now.

  3. Download JSON or Export CSV

    Instantly view the extracted product data as formatted JSON, copy it, or download it as a .json or .csv file.
